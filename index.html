Weather

Weather provides information on various parameters like temperature, humidity, clouds, rainfall, snow or storms, and time of sunset, etc. with reference to a region. These parameters and their day-to-day changes make up the weather and climate. Weather may change from day to day and even hour to hour.

🌡️ Temperature

The maximum temperature of the day at a given place normally occurs in the afternoon because the Sun's rays heat the Earth's surface for several hours.


At night, the Sun's rays do not fall on that place, so it cools down. The minimum temperature usually occurs in the early morning.

💧 Humidity

Humidity is measured by an instrument called a hygrometer. A hygrometer measures humidity, which helps us understand the chances of rainfall.

🌧️ Rainfall

Rainfall is measured by an instrument called a Rain Gauge.

Climate

The weather pattern of a place can be seen from the weather records of the past several years. The average weather pattern of a place over a long period of time, say 25 years, is called the climate of that place.

🌡️ Average Temperature

Climate describes the usual temperature conditions of a place over a long period of time. Some places have hot climates, while others have cold climates.

🌧️ Rainfall

The amount and pattern of rainfall over many years are important parts of a place's climate. Some regions receive heavy rainfall, while others are dry.

🌬️ Winds

The direction and pattern of winds over a long period also help describe the climate of a place.

🍂 Seasons

Climate influences the seasons experienced in a region. Different places may have different seasonal patterns throughout the year.
